Our Story

SNA Platform was developed by Emre Yasin Yıldan and Aboubacar Sow as part of the 2025-2026 Yazlab II course. The project aims to eliminate the complexities in the field of social network analysis, enabling researchers, students, and curious users to understand the structure of social networks.

In this era where human relationships are increasingly important in the digital world, understanding the dynamics of social networks is crucial. We built the SNA Platform to make this analysis accessible, user-friendly, and visually comprehensible. By transforming complex datasets into interactive graphs, we enable every user to deeply understand social networks.

Our vision is to democratize social network analysis and make this powerful tool accessible to everyone. We aim to build a platform that serves a wide range of users from academia to practice, from education to research.
